# Trademor

Trademor is a growth and export partner for Pakistani manufacturers, suppliers, SMEs and business owners. The supplied brand guide identifies Trademor as an official Alibaba.com channel partner. The website copy also names Odoo, Blooming and Payoneer as partnerships.

Visitors should understand how Trademor helps them reach buyers, enter new markets, sell globally, manage operations and receive global payments. The main conversion is a short business enquiry. The user confirmed that a website form is sufficient; no CRM or inbox destination has been provided.

The supplied content covers Home and Alibaba.com only. Other service pages, case studies, contact addresses, phone numbers and partner logo assets have not been supplied. Do not invent these. The business statistics and prices are user-supplied claims, not independently verified results.

Brand commitments: preserve the supplied logo, its growth arrow, black and orange (#FF7300), and Sora typography. The user requested Impeccable guidance and Mobbin references; Impeccable is accessible via its official GitHub source, while Mobbin tools are unavailable.

The local Express backend stores requests privately in JSONL; it does not email them. The Netlify build uses Netlify Forms instead of running Express, with submissions managed in the site's Forms dashboard. Neither setup sends email automatically. This project is a responsive English web application, not a native app.
